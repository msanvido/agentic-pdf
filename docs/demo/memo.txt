INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:       All Engineering Staff
FROM:     Dana Whitfield, VP Platform Engineering
DATE:     August 14, 2026
SUBJECT:  On-call rotation changes effective September 1
PRIORITY: High — action required before August 28

SUMMARY

Starting September 1 we are moving from a per-team on-call model to a
follow-the-sun rotation across the Austin, Dublin, and Singapore sites.
This memo explains what changes, what stays the same, and the three actions
each engineer must complete before August 28.

WHAT CHANGES

- Primary paging follows the sun: Austin covers 14:00–22:00 UTC, Dublin
  covers 07:00–15:00 UTC, and Singapore covers 23:00–07:00 UTC.
- Escalation paths are consolidated into a single PagerDuty policy named
  platform-tier-1. The legacy team-level policies will be retired on
  September 15.
- Compensation: on-call shifts carry a 4% differential, paid monthly.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE

Severity definitions, the incident command handbook, and postmortem SLAs
(postmortems due within five business days for SEV-1/SEV-2) remain exactly
as documented in the engineering handbook, section 7.

REQUIRED ACTIONS BY AUGUST 28

1. Confirm your home site and timezone in Workday.
2. Complete the updated incident command training (45 minutes) in LMS.
3. Acknowledge the new rotation calendar in PagerDuty.

QUESTIONS

Bring questions to Thursday's platform all-hands, or ping
#on-call-transition. Exceptions (e.g., medical restrictions) go directly
to your manager and to Dana.
