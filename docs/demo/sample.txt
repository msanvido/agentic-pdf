ACME QUARTERLY REPORT — Q3 2026
===============================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Revenue for Q3 2026 reached $4.2M, up 18% quarter over quarter. The API
platform now serves 12,000 daily active developers. Churn fell to 2.1%
following the launch of the retention program in July.

KEY METRICS

- Revenue: $4.2M (+18% QoQ)
- Daily active developers: 12,000 (+31%)
- Net revenue retention: 118%
- Gross margin: 79%

PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

The Agentic Gateway shipped to general availability in August. Early
adoption exceeded forecasts; 340 enterprise accounts enabled the feature
within the first three weeks. Latency at p99 improved from 420ms to 180ms
after the edge caching rollout.

RISKS AND CONCERNS

Hiring for the platform team remains behind plan. Two senior SRE roles
have been open since May. Regulatory review of the EU data residency
program is expected to conclude in November.

OUTLOOK

We forecast $4.8M revenue in Q4 driven by seasonal enterprise renewals and
continued adoption of the usage-based pricing model introduced this
quarter.
